# Executive Summary

WealthBridge Advisors wanted to understand how demographics, investment
goals and risk appetite influence investment decisions. Using survey
data from **40 investors**, I cleaned and transformed the data in
**Excel Power Query**, modelled it in **Power BI**, and built an
interactive dashboard.

The analysis revealed an important behavioural insight: although
respondents stated **Returns** as their primary decision factor, the
most popular investment avenue was **PPF**, indicating a preference for
safety over higher returns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Key Insights

## Returns vs Reality

Although respondents ranked **Returns** as their top decision factor,
**PPF** received the highest investment allocation.

## Core Customer Segment

The **26--30** age group was the largest and most active investor
segment.

## Gender Differences

Male investors participated more across most investment avenues.

## Investment Goals

Retirement planning was the dominant savings objective with a preferred
duration of **3--5 years**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recommendations

-   Target the 26--30 age segment.
-   Lead marketing with returns while communicating risk clearly.
-   Design products around medium-term retirement planning.
-   Develop tailored campaigns for different demographic groups.
